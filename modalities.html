Training Programs

Find the perfect path for your Aikido journey

Beginner Classes

Perfect for those new to Aikido or martial arts in general. Our beginner program focuses on fundamental movements, basic techniques, and dojo etiquette in a supportive, non-intimidating environment.

What You'll Learn:

  • Basic stances and footwork (tai no henko)
  • Fundamental throws and pins
  • Proper falling techniques (ukemi)
  • Breathing and centering exercises
  • Dojo etiquette and traditions
  • Introduction to Aikido philosophy

Schedule: Tuesday & Thursday 7:00-8:30 PM, Saturday 10:00-11:30 AM

Duration: 8-week introductory course, then ongoing classes

Intermediate Training

For students who have mastered the basics and are ready to deepen their practice. Intermediate classes explore more complex techniques and begin to emphasize the internal aspects of Aikido.

Focus Areas:

  • Advanced throwing techniques (nage-waza)
  • Joint locks and immobilizations (katame-waza)
  • Multiple attacker scenarios (randori)
  • Weapons training introduction (jo and bokken)
  • Ki development exercises
  • Partner sensitivity training

Schedule: Monday & Wednesday 7:00-8:30 PM, Saturday 11:45 AM-1:15 PM

Prerequisites: Completion of beginner program or equivalent experience

Advanced Practice

For experienced practitioners seeking to refine their technique and deepen their understanding of Aikido's subtle principles. Advanced classes emphasize spontaneous response, advanced weapons work, and teaching preparation.

Advanced Elements:

  • Free-form practice (jiyu-waza)
  • Advanced weapons forms (kata)
  • Teaching methodology
  • Historical and philosophical studies
  • Demonstration preparation
  • Leadership development

Schedule: Friday 7:00-8:30 PM, Sunday 2:00-3:30 PM

Prerequisites: Minimum 3rd kyu rank or instructor approval

Weapons Training (Bukiwaza)

Traditional Aikido weapons training using wooden sword (bokken), staff (jo), and knife (tanto). Weapons practice enhances understanding of distance, timing, and the geometric principles underlying empty-hand techniques.

Weapons Covered:

  • Bokken (Wooden Sword): Basic cuts, thrusts, and partner exercises
  • Jo (4-foot Staff): Fundamental strikes, blocks, and kata
  • Tanto (Wooden Knife): Defense against knife attacks
  • Partner Practice: Coordinated exercises developing timing and distance

Schedule: Saturday 9:00-10:00 AM (all levels), Wednesday 8:30-9:30 PM (intermediate+)

Equipment: Available for purchase at the dojo or through recommended suppliers

Youth Program

Specially designed for children and teenagers (ages 8-16), our youth program teaches Aikido principles through age-appropriate activities that build confidence, respect, and self-discipline.

Youth Benefits:

  • Improved focus and concentration
  • Physical fitness and coordination
  • Conflict resolution skills
  • Respect for others and self
  • Cultural awareness and appreciation
  • Leadership opportunities

Schedule: Saturday 1:30-2:30 PM

Class Size: Limited to 12 students for personalized attention

Special Workshops & Seminars

Throughout the year, we host special events featuring visiting instructors, intensive training sessions, and cultural workshops that deepen understanding of Aikido's rich heritage.

Regular Offerings:

  • Monthly intensive training sessions
  • Visiting master instructor seminars
  • Women's self-defense workshops
  • Meditation and breathing workshops
  • Japanese culture and language sessions
  • Annual summer retreat

Registration: Open to all students; some workshops welcome non-members

Private Lessons

One-on-one instruction tailored to individual needs and goals. Private lessons are ideal for accelerated learning, addressing specific challenges, or preparing for rank examinations.

Benefits: Personalized attention, flexible scheduling, customized curriculum

Availability: By appointment with qualified instructors

Rates: Contact the dojo for current pricing
