Directions & Parking

Our dojo is conveniently located in the heart of Peaceful Valley, easily accessible by car or public transportation.

By Car:

From Highway 101: Take Exit 15 (Harmony Boulevard), turn east and continue for 2 miles. Turn right on Peaceful Street, then left on Harmony Way. The dojo is on your right in the Zen Gardens complex.

Parking:

Free parking is available in our dedicated lot behind the building. Additional street parking is available on Harmony Way and surrounding streets.

Public Transportation:

Bus routes 12 and 34 stop at Harmony Boulevard & Peaceful Street, just a 3-minute walk from the dojo.

What to Expect on Your First Visit

We want your first experience at our dojo to be comfortable and welcoming. Here's what you can expect:

  • Arrival: Please arrive 15 minutes early to complete any necessary paperwork and get oriented
  • Observation: You're welcome to watch an entire class to get a feel for our teaching style
  • Trial Participation: If you'd like to try a class, we'll pair you with an experienced student who will guide you through basic movements
  • Equipment: We provide loaner equipment for trial classes - just bring comfortable workout clothes
  • Questions: Our instructors and students are happy to answer any questions before, during, or after class
  • No Pressure: Take your time deciding if Aikido is right for you - there's no obligation to join immediately
